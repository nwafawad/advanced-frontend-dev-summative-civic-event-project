Accessibility statement

Last reviewed: November 2026

CivicEvents+ should be usable by every resident, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, screen magnification or captions. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we do

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Pages use headings, landmarks and labels so screen readers can move around them. Messages and errors are read out.
  • Every form field has a label, and errors say what to fix next to the field.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG AA colour contrast.
  • Promo videos show captions by default, with a Captions on/off button and a written transcript.
  • Audio and video use the browser's own player, which works with the keyboard. If a file cannot play, you get a download link.
  • The dashboard chart also has a table with the same numbers for screen readers.
  • Animations stop when your device's "reduce motion" setting is on.
  • The layout works on screens from 360px wide, from phones to desktops.

Known limitations

  • Audio announcements do not have transcripts yet.
  • Promo captions are made from the promo's caption text and spread evenly over the video, so they may not match the speech exactly. The full text is always in the transcript under the video.
  • Event images are decorative and have no description. All event details are given in the text.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, or you need information in another format, email help@civicevents.com. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will help.
